# Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 26 November 2026_

FinGist ("we", "us") is a financial news app for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: **you do not need an account, we do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not show ads or sell data.**

## 1. Information stored only on your phone

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us:

- Saved (bookmarked) stories
- Recent searches
- Your app settings and recently loaded news, kept so the app works offline

Deleting the app removes this information.

## 2. News alerts (only if you turn them on)

If you turn on news alerts, the app sends us:

- a **push token**: a random code from Apple or Google that lets us deliver notifications to the app on your phone
- your phone type (iOS or Android)
- your alert settings (India, Global or All, and quiet hours)

We also store how many alerts we sent you today, to keep to the daily limit. We use this information only to send you news alerts. When you turn alerts off in the app, we delete your push token. We also delete it automatically if Apple or Google tells us it is no longer valid (for example, after you delete the app).

## 3. Searching and reading news

When you load news or search, the app sends your request (for example, the search words) to our server to fetch matching stories. We do not link searches to you or to your phone. Search history is kept only on your phone (see section 1).

## 4. Information we do not collect

- No name, email, phone number or account
- No location, contacts, photos or files
- No advertising identifier and no tracking across other apps or websites
- No analytics or advertising tools

## 5. Service providers

We use these providers to run FinGist:

- **Cloudflare** hosts our server and database. Like any web server, it processes the IP address of each request for delivery and security.
- **Expo** (650 Industries), **Apple** and **Google** deliver push notifications using your push token.
- **Unsplash** and **Pexels** serve the representative card photos. Your phone loads these images directly from them, so they receive your IP address.
- **Google Gemini** writes the news summaries. It receives only publicly available news headlines and descriptions, never information about you.
- **News publishers.** When you tap "Read full story", the publisher's website opens and its own privacy policy applies.

## 6. How long we keep information

Push tokens and alert settings are kept while alerts are on, and deleted when you turn alerts off or the token stops working. Server request logs are kept by our hosting provider for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

## 7. Children

FinGist is a general financial news service and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Your choices and rights

- Turn alerts on or off at any time in the app (☰ More → News alerts), or in your phone's notification settings.
- Delete local data by deleting the app.
